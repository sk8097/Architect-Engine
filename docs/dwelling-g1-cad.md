# Measured G+1 house CAD

Open **New Project → Residential → House / low-rise dwelling → Independent House** (or Detached House, Duplex House or Villa), then **Automatic drawing → G+1 house with measured indoor staircase**. Enter two floors for one household, rectangular measured plot and common floor plate, offsets, cardinal facing, and supplied wall, passage and door dimensions. Choose inside access, straight stair, private upper floor and no lift. This route supports optional measured open-surface parking but not an accessible terrace.

Supply clear stair width, floor-to-floor rise, riser count, going and depth of each end landing. The app computes a plan-view straight stair and its reserve, places it at the same coordinates on both floor sheets, and connects each passage to its corresponding landing. Riser height is the input height divided by riser count; the represented run is (risers − 1) × going. The number and dimensions are supplied design assumptions, not app-derived code limits. The DXF includes a `STAIRS` layer with up/down direction.

Assign each measured room selection to Ground or First floor. Quantities of a room type all go on its chosen floor. Include one closed kitchen and living room on the selected main-kitchen floor, measured shared bathrooms and the requested bedroom counts. The layout keeps clear room size, checks plot, supplied setbacks and cumulative FAR/FSI, room/door fit and reachability. Required adjacency, separation or outside-edge conflicts stop export rather than being ignored. A valid result opens both sheets automatically, with editable DXF exports for one or both sheets. Reopen the result from Projects.

This is a concept for a single household, with one common rectangular plate and an indoor straight stair. A plan view is not a verified stair section or construction detail. Actual slab opening, headroom, structure, fire/egress, handrails and local code require review. Covered or structured parking, external/shared stairs, independent rental units, ensuite access, open kitchen layouts, different upper plates and terrace access are not generated by this template.

## Optional open-surface parking

Choose **Car parking**, **Two-wheeler parking**, or **Car + two-wheeler parking**. Enter bay counts under **Operations**; enter clear bay dimensions, vehicle strip offset measured left-to-right while looking in from the street, approach depth, pedestrian width and separation gap in the concept step. The parking area allowance covers all bays and the full-width vehicle approach, not the pedestrian path. Confirm local permission and unobstructed vehicle access across the full width of the strip. A setback input alone does not establish that parking is allowed there.

Bays are drawn in one row, cars followed by two-wheelers, with a full-width approach directly from the frontage. The pedestrian path runs separately from frontage to the entire ground-floor opening. The ground sheet and combined DXF carry `PARKING_BAYS`, `VEHICLE_APPROACH` and `PEDESTRIAN_APPROACH`. The first-floor sheet does not repeat this geometry. If the bank or path exceeds the plot, conflicts with the house or other access, or exceeds the supplied parking allowance, no sheet is exported. The concept does not model turning, curb cuts, individual gates, grade, drainage, accessible bays or EV equipment.
