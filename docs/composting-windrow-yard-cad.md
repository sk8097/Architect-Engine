# Composting windrow yard CAD

Choose **Infrastructure / Public Works → Public realm / landscape / waste → Composting Facility → Automatic drawing → Composting yard: measured windrows and geometric volume**. Use Support Facility or leave the arrangement blank. This template covers one open yard without buildings, parking or separate Space selections.

Enter a cardinally aligned rectangular or square plot. Width is west–east; depth is south–north. Offsets use the southwest plot corner, and setbacks use west as left and south as front. The layout shows **1–30 identical north–south piles**, clear aisles measured between pile toes, outer side strips and independently sized north/south headlands. For one pile use zero internal aisle width. All measured geometry must fit the supplied setbacks; paths are reserves only, not vehicle tracks.

Supply base and centred flat-top widths and lengths, and pile height. The top must be narrower and shorter than the base, so all four faces taper. The plan shows the two rectangles and the sloping corner edges. The true-scale transverse section passes through the middle of the piles on a common flat datum. The geometric volume uses the exact prismoidal formula `height × (base area + 4 × mid-height area + top area) / 6` and must meet the supplied volume target (enter zero for no minimum). It is neither mass nor process throughput. Clear yard area excludes the entire pile footprints.

Example: on a **40 × 55 m** plot, place four **4 × 30 m** base piles with **2 × 24 m** tops and **2 m** height. With **3 m** aisles, **2 m** side strips and **5 m / 6 m** south/north headlands the envelope is **29 × 41 m**, with **709 m²** of clear area. The piles have **164 m³** each, **656 m³** total. These are geometric example dimensions, not operational guidance.

Valid briefs preview and export separate editable layered DXFs for the plan and section. Reopen under **Composting windrow yard CAD** in Projects. A failed fit or volume check preserves the brief but blocks both exports. No pile stability, equipment turning, process performance, pad grading, drainage, leachate, odour or permit design is included.
