# Measured passenger shelter canopy CAD

Choose **Institutional & Public Utility → Sports / recreation / public amenities → Passenger Shelter**. Under **Automatic drawing**, select **Passenger shelter: measured canopy and headroom**. Use Outdoor or Indoor + Outdoor, or leave Activity setting blank. Leave enclosed floors, parking and separate Space selections empty. This mode draws an open-sided canopy, not a bus stop or a terminal.

Use a cardinally aligned rectangular or square plot. Plot width is west–east, depth south–north; west is left and south is front for setbacks. The offsets locate the southwest corner of the **roof projection**, not the post grid. Enter 1–8 equal north–south bays, each with the same centre spacing. Both post rows use the same square footprint. Roof projections are measured from the outer post centre lines; they must cover the entire outer post footprints. Clear walking width is the centre-to-centre width between rows minus one post width.

The roof soffit drops in a single straight plane from the high west eave to the low east eave. Enter both levels above one flat grade datum and the **vertical** depth to the top plane. The lowest headroom in the clear walking strip occurs at the east inner post face. The low east eave headroom is checked separately. The full roof projection must fit the setbacks; its high top must meet any supplied height limit. Sloping plane area excludes gutters, seams and material waste.

**Geometry example:** A 12 × 18 m plot, roof projection 2 m from west and 3 m from south. Three **3.20 m** bays, **3.60 m** between rows and **0.20 m** posts. Project the roof **1.00 m west**, **0.80 m east** and **0.60 m** past each end. With eave soffits **3.10 m west / 2.50 m east** and **0.18 m** vertical depth, the **5.400 × 10.800 m** roof covers a **3.400 m** clear strip. The strip has **2.600 m** minimum headroom and the roof plane drops **11.111%** eastward. High roof top is **3.280 m**; sloping area is **58.679 m²**. The example is not a design standard.

Export separate editable layered DXFs for the **plan** and **true-scale typical transverse section**, in local millimetres. Reopen in Projects using **Passenger shelter canopy CAD**. Failed checks preserve the brief with a specific reason and block both exports, without generic fallback. The template does not verify roof structure or material, wind uplift, foundations, drainage, guards, passenger loading, approach grades or accessibility compliance.
