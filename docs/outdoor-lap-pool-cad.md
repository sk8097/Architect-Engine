# Outdoor lap pool CAD

Choose **Institutional & Public Utility → Sports / recreation / public amenities → Swimming Pool → Automatic drawing → Outdoor lap pool: measured lanes, deck and floor section**. Use the Outdoor configuration or leave it unset. This is a single outdoor pool without buildings, parking or separate Space selections.

The plot must be a cardinally aligned rectangle or square. Plot width runs west to east, plot depth runs south to north, and the shallow end is always south. The deck offsets are measured from the west and south plot edges. Setbacks use south as front and west as left.

Supply the clear water length, 1–12 identical lane widths (enter a single width for all lanes), margin beyond the outer lanes on each side, clear deck widths and offsets. The water width is **lane count × lane width + two side margins**. The deck envelope includes both side strips and the two end strips, and must fit all supplied setbacks. Lines in the water show plan lane divisions and floor grade breaks, not equipment or physical barriers.

For the longitudinal section, supply shallow and deep water depths and the length of the flat floor at each end. The remaining length must be positive; it forms one straight transition. The actual grade is the depth difference divided by the transition length, checked against your supplied maximum, not against any standard. The volume uses a constant clear width and the exact area of the longitudinal water section (two rectangles and a trapezoid). The section uses water level as local zero, with no vertical exaggeration or assumed deck elevation.

Example: on a **24 × 39 m** plot, use **six 2 m lanes**, **0.5 m margins**, **25 m length**, **2 m side decks** and **2.5 m end decks**. The water area is **13 × 25 m**, and the deck envelope is **17 × 30 m** at (3, 4) m. With depths of **1.2 m** and **1.8 m** and flats of **8 m** and **5 m**, the **12 m** transition has **5.000% grade** and the pool holds **475.800 m³**. This meets a supplied 5% limit for the example only; these values are not recommendations.

Save to preview the plan and true-scale section and export each as a separate editable layered DXF in millimetres. Reopen **Outdoor lap pool CAD** from Projects. Failed fit or floor checks preserve the brief but block both exports. The drawings do not design or certify the shell, deck elevations, drainage, water treatment, equipment, barriers, accessibility, diving or swimming safety.
