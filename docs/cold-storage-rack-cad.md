# Cold-storage pallet rack CAD

Choose **Industrial → Warehouse / logistics → Cold Storage** (or **Freezer Warehouse**) → **Automatic drawing → Cold storage: measured pallet racks and bay schedule**. Use Rack Storage or leave the arrangement blank. Set one floor; use one chamber and, where available, one temperature zone. Leave parking and separate Space selections empty. Under the Facility inputs, use **Required clear height** for the available unobstructed height from finished floor to the lowest overhead restriction. Optional **Pallet positions** is treated as a minimum nominal count for this concept.

Supply a cardinally aligned rectangular or square plot. Plot width is west–east and depth south–north. Chamber offsets are from the southwest corner. Setbacks use west as left and south as front. The chamber must fit supplied setbacks and any FSI/coverage limits; the full chamber footprint is used for the single-floor fit check.

There are two outer single racks and **0–8** interior back-to-back pairs, with identical north–south bays. Enter clear widths for the working aisles, the space between paired backs (zero if none), the outside wall gaps, staging and the rear cross-aisle. The south wall opening aligns with the first aisle from the west through the front staging band. No door details or equipment turns are drawn. Keep the staging/bypass geometry clear of equipment and stored goods if using it as a planning reserve.

For every bay, the submitted pallet width times slot count plus gaps between loads and at both ends must fit the clear opening; loaded depth cannot exceed rack depth. The first load level is at the floor, followed by equally spaced levels if applicable. The loaded pallet height must be below the level pitch for multiple levels. The top load height plus supplied overhead clearance must fit the Facility clear height. These are static envelopes only; beams, decks, anchorage and clearances required by equipment, fire protection or refrigeration are not designed by the app.

Example: use a **20 × 24 m** plot, one interior pair, three **2.6 m** bays per row with **0.10 m** uprights, two pallets per bay and three load levels. With **1.2 m** rack depth, **3.2 m** clear aisles, **0.3 m** back-to-back and wall gaps, **3 m** staging, **2 m** rear cross-aisle and **0.2 m** walls, the chamber is **12.5 × 13.6 m**. For **1.1 × 1.1 × 1.4 m** loaded pallet envelopes, **0.10 m** bay gaps and **1.8 m** level pitch, the total is **72 nominal positions**, with the top load at **5.0 m** and **0.8 m** remaining below a **5.8 m** clear height. These are example inputs, not standards.

Save to preview and export layered editable DXF in millimetres and a CSV with each bay's position, load-facing direction and nominal count. Reopen from Projects under **Cold-storage rack CAD**. Failed checks keep the brief but block both exports. No rack load rating, thermal design, power, refrigeration, fire clearance, equipment tracking or compliance is verified.
