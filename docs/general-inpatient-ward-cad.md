# General inpatient ward CAD

Choose **Institutional & Public Utility → Healthcare → Hospital, General Hospital or Nursing Home** and **Inpatient** as the care model. Under **Automatic drawing**, choose **General ward: measured bed bays and nursing access**. This is one new ground-floor ward, not a whole hospital. Leave separate Space selections empty; the drawing provides the ward, nursing lobby, counter and washroom reserve directly.

Enter the measured envelope, setbacks/offsets and cardinal entrance, enclosure thickness, door opening and clear aisle. Enter an even patient bed count from **2 to 20**, and the bed footprint, access on both sides of each bed and clear foot space. Each footprint connects via its foot clearance to the central aisle; clear spaces at either end span the room width. The front entrance and ward door align with the aisle. An enclosed washroom reserve opens off the front lobby. The nursing counter faces the lobby aisle, with a working pocket and clear access around its end.

A geometry-only example is a **10.4 × 20 m** envelope, 0.2 m walls, 1.4 m door, 4.2 m nursing lobby and 2.4 m central aisle. Eight 1.1 × 2.2 m bed footprints fit with 0.8 m on both sides, 1.15 m foot clearances and a minimum 1.6 m clear at both ends. The front lobby can fit a 2.4 × 0.65 m counter and a 1 m working pocket. These are sample inputs, not clinical or accessibility standards.

**Save** generates the preview and editable DXF or reports what does not fit. The brief is retained either way; invalid geometry is not exported as generic zoning. Reopen **General inpatient ward CAD** from Projects. Beds, the counter, access aisles and furniture clearances are separate DXF layers.

The washroom is an enclosure only: there are no sanitary fixtures or plumbing. No ICU, isolation, treatment, medical services, bed-transfer manoeuvre, clinical standards, accessibility, egress, lighting, or ventilation verification is included.
