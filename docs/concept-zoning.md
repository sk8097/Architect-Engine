# Measured concept zoning CAD

New Project routes all eight categories and 34 subcategories to the CAD workflow. Existing detailed house and high-rise routes stay separate. Other documented types have a **concept zoning** route rather than a claim of a specialist building or engineering generator. The existing central taxonomy drives the 30 program policies and access-side preferences.

For a parcel, enter a measured rectangle or square and select at least one listed or unlisted space. In Space details, enter its measured/required clear width and length in metres. Enter quantities, level, required envelope edge and adjacency/separation where applicable. An exact clear width and depth are kept, and drawings are allocated inside the plot minus supplied setbacks. Upper floors with zones require an explicitly sized rectangular zoning envelope; the sheet shares the ground model origin but does not validate vertical structure or connections. Only levels with requested zones receive sheets.

For a corridor type, supply measured route length, right-of-way and confirm a straight measured alignment. The generator produces a linear envelope with station dimensions. It does not invent a geographic bearing, bends, grade, road lanes or surveyed alignment. Optional measured zones can be placed within the width, but do not constitute a road facility design.

Saving opens validated geometry or reports the blocker after persisting the inputs. Exact mandatory edges and touching adjacencies are checked; separation means no shared edge, not a certified clearance. Access preferences influence search order, not a site's legal entrance. The bounded rectangular packing search does not prove infeasibility. Preview and editable layered DXF share the same geometry, labelled as concept zoning. No partitions, detailed openings, service design, fire, accessibility, parking operation, structural or authority checks are inferred. Because this is clear-zone planning, it does not validate gross floor area or FSI and must not be relied on for permits or construction.
