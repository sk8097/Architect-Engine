# Warehouse opposing cross-dock CAD

**New Project → Industrial & Warehousing → Warehouse / logistics → Cross-Dock → Automatic drawing → Cross-dock: opposing inbound and outbound banks**. Also available for General Warehouse and Distribution Centre. Use one new rectangular ground-floor shell. Leave Storage layout as Not specified; this concept does not design rack storage. Select the inbound cardinal frontage and confirm measured access at the inbound boundary, the opposite outbound boundary and the left staff boundary when looking in from the inbound side.

Enter **Docks / loading doors** as the total across both banks, then enter the inbound count. The remainder are outbound; both sides must contain at least one dock. Supply independent bay, door, approach, manoeuvring reserve and internal staging dimensions for each bank. The inbound approach sets the distance from the inbound boundary to the reserve; the outbound route extends to the opposite boundary and must be at least the supplied clear depth. Enter shell and wall dimensions, offset, staff aisle and door widths, the clear freight-lane width, and your vehicle dimension reference. Leave separate space selections empty; dock, staging and route zones come from these measurements.

Save produces both dock banks and openings, a marked internal freight route between staging strips, and a side staff walk into a walled aisle. The aisle has a door into the freight hall; interior pedestrian safety and separation outside that aisle are not designed. A failed clearance or approach fit gives a reason and stops DXF export instead of substituting the older single-bank layout. Reopen via **Projects → Warehouse cross-dock CAD**. The DXF has distinct layers for the inbound bank, outbound bank, staging, transfer lane, dock openings and staff approach.

All vehicle footprints are static measured reserves. No swept path, reversing or turning verification, street connection, dock elevation or leveler design, ramps, truck parking, racks, staff facilities, fire exits, or jurisdictional approval are provided. Obtain specialist review before construction or operation.
