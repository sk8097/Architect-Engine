# Surveyed pedestrian underpass CAD

Choose **Infrastructure / Public Works → Transport infrastructure → Underpass**. Under **Underpass drawing**, select **Surveyed pedestrian underpass: grade and headroom**. Leave Arrangement blank or select Linear Corridor. Leave separate Space selections empty.

Enter the projected metre-grid reference and two straight walkway centreline controls A and B, up to 2 km apart. Use one elevation datum. In **Site / plot / land**, enter a `chainage,floor level` walkway list and a separate `chainage,underside level` soffit list (2–80 increasing stations in each, metres, separated by semicolons or line breaks). Chainage starts at A. The walkway list includes both uncovered approaches. The first and last soffit stations are the portals and must lie strictly inside the walkway span. The two sample lists do not have to share stations.

The vertical profiles are piecewise linear. The minimum headroom is the smallest `soffit level − floor level` at the union of both profiles’ breakpoints inside the covered span, including the portals. It is vertical **along the centreline only**. Walkway grade is checked for each entire segment, including the two uncovered approaches. The plan checks the clear width against the submitted minimum, and clear width plus both covered-span wall envelopes against the total centred corridor width. Wall thickness is a plan envelope, not a structural specification.

**Example:** A `(500000,2000000)`, B `(500060,2000080)`. Walkway `5,100.8;25,99.8;45,99.6;65,99.8;85,100.8` and soffit `23,102.5;35,102.25;55,102.25;67,102.5` produce an **80 m** route with a **44 m** covered span. Minimum vertical headroom is **2.550 m**, first occurring at **Ch 35 m**. Maximum absolute walkway grade is **5.000%**. A **4.0 m** clear width and two **0.30 m** wall envelopes fit in a **5.0 m** corridor. The example is not a design standard.

Export separate editable layered DXFs for the **projected plan** and the **true-scale longitudinal profile**. Profile X is chainage, and Y measures height above the labelled local RL zero; both are in millimetres. Reopen through Projects → **Surveyed pedestrian underpass CAD**. Failed checks preserve the brief and show a reason without offering either export or a generic fallback. No transverse headroom, crossfall, obstructions, accessible landings, drainage, flood, lighting, traffic or structural approval is assessed.
