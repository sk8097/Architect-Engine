# Surveyed waterfront promenade CAD

Choose **Infrastructure / Public Works → Public realm / landscape / waste → Waterfront Promenade**. In **Waterfront drawing**, choose **Surveyed promenade: path and lookout pads**. This is a survey-only mode; use Linear Corridor or Public Open Space (or leave arrangement blank) and leave separate Space selections empty.

In **Site**, choose a projected metre grid and enter two Easting,Northing baseline controls A and B, and the direction of landward offsets when looking A to B. Along the baseline, enter 2–80 `chainage,shoreline offset,inland limit offset` records. Chainage starts at A. Offsets are perpendicular to A–B; positive offsets are toward land, so a baseline away from the shore may have negative shore or path offsets. Use a newline or semicolon between records. The sample chainages must increase; the first and last define the path ends. Between samples both boundaries are piecewise linear in plan.

Enter the signed offset to the shore-facing path edge, its clear width, and minimum gaps to shore and inland limit. Add 1–12 pads, using `ID,start chainage,end chainage,shoreward projection`. IDs are unique, start with a letter, and contain only letters, digits, underscores and hyphens (up to 16 characters). List pads in order without overlap. Pads widen the path on its shore side without interrupting the clear path. The app checks both pad ends and all intervening shore breakpoints; a pad cannot mask a locally narrow gap. **All gaps are measured at equal chainage, normal to the baseline, not as shortest distances to the shoreline.**

**Geometry example:** A `(1000,2000)` to B `(1060,2080)` with positive offsets on the left. Limit records `10,-1,13;40,2,12;70,0,11;100,-2,14`, path offset 5 m and width 3 m. With pads `L1,20,55,1;L2,75,90,2`, the **90 m** path covers **270 m²** and the pads add **65 m²**. The minimum baseline-normal shore gap is **2.000 m** (inside L1 at Ch 40 m) and the minimum inland gap is **3.000 m**. These are sample dimensions, not setback or safety recommendations.

The editable layered DXF uses projected millimetres. The CSV gives each pad’s chainage interval, projection, minimum baseline-normal shore gap and area. Reopen under **Surveyed promenade CAD** in Projects. A failed check keeps the brief with a reason but no DXF, CSV or generic fallback. No flood, erosion, tidal level, statutory coastal setback, guarding, structure, grade, drainage or accessibility is verified.
