# Hotel guestroom wing CAD

Choose **Commercial → Hospitality / lodging → Hotel, Boutique Hotel, Business Hotel, Budget Hotel, Motel or Guest House → Automatic drawing → Hotel wing: mirrored guestrooms and shared corridor**. Use a measured rectangular or square plot, cardinal frontage and one ground floor. Leave separate Space selections, parking and terrace requests empty. In Facility, enter **2–20 even guestroom keys**, all the same standard, double or king layout; suites and other room types are not drawn.

Enter the gross wing width, wall and door dimensions and two plot offsets. The gross depth comes from the measured module length along the corridor: `keys/2 × clear module length + (keys/2 + 1) × wall thickness`. Each mirrored room opens off the central corridor into its own vestibule. The vestibule has separate doors to the sleeping room and bathroom reserve. Each sleeping room has an exterior side-wall window. Enter its width and the measured bed and clearances. The app checks the enclosing walls, door connections and measured fit rather than substituting smaller fixtures or dropping rooms.

Example: on a **28 × 28 m** plot, enter **six keys**, a **16.4 m** gross width, 4 m left and 5 m front offsets, **0.2 m** walls, **1.0 m** doors, a **4.4 m** clear module length and **2.0 m** minimum corridor. Enter **2.0 m** vestibule length and **2.4 m** service-strip width, a **1.8 × 2.1 m** bed with **0.7 m** beside and **0.9 m** at the foot, plus a **2.0 m** side-wall window. The resulting footprint is **16.4 × 14.0 m**; each bathroom reserve is **2.4 × 2.2 m**.

Save to preview and share an editable, layered **DXF**. Reopen **Hotel guestroom wing CAD** in Projects. If a measured check fails, the brief stays saved with the reason, without a DXF or generic zoning substitute. This is a concept for one wing, not a complete hotel. No reception, housekeeping, accessible guestroom design, secondary egress, sanitary fixtures, MEP or regulatory approval is included.
