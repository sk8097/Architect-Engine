# Twin-cell ground-level water tank CAD

Choose **Infrastructure / Public Works → Water / wastewater / stormwater → Water Tank** or **Ground Reservoir** → **Automatic drawing → Twin-cell tank: measured storage and single-cell reserve**. Use Storage or leave the arrangement blank. The template is one ground-level tank with two cells and no building floors, parking or separate Space selections.

Use a cardinally aligned rectangular or square plot. Width is west–east, depth is south–north, and offsets are from the southwest plot corner. Setbacks use west as left and south as front. Enter the tank's clear cell widths and common length, perimeter and divider thickness, and clear service strip. The offsets locate the outside of the **service envelope**, which must fit within supplied setbacks.

Enter the common floor-to-wall-top height and, for each cell, its lower usable level and normal water level **above that common floor**. Normal level must exceed lower usable level and leave the submitted minimum geometric freeboard. The usable volume of a cell is its clear rectangular area times the difference between those two water levels. The combined capacity is the sum; the **worst-case capacity with either one cell offline is the smaller of the two usable volumes**. Check each against an independent supplied target; enter zero for no minimum. The calculation assumes the remaining cell can operate independently. It does not establish that the system can isolate a cell or maintain supply during an outage.

Example: on a **22 × 24 m** plot, set **5 m and 4 m** clear widths, **10 m** clear length, **0.25 m** perimeter walls, a **0.30 m** divider and **1.5 m** service space. The **9.8 × 10.5 m** tank has a **12.8 × 13.5 m** service envelope. With a **3.5 m** wall top, A's **0.4–3.1 m** range holds **135 m³**, and B's **0.3–2.8 m** range holds **100 m³**. That's **235 m³** combined and **100 m³** worst-case one-cell reserve. These are geometric example inputs, not design standards.

Valid briefs export separate editable layered DXFs in millimetres for the plan and true-scale transverse section. Reopen from Projects under **Twin-cell ground tank CAD**. Failed checks preserve the brief, show a reason and block both exports. The section shows wall and level geometry only, with no base/roof thickness, structural, waterproofing, water quality, pipework, isolation, overflow or access design.
