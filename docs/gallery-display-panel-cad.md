# Gallery display-panel CAD

Choose **Institutional & Public Utility → Culture / worship / community → Museum**, **Gallery** or **Art Centre**. Under **Automatic drawing** choose **Gallery: double-sided display panels and clear viewing zones**. Use Main Hall or leave the arrangement blank, set **one floor above grade**, and leave parking and separate Space selections empty.

Use a cardinally aligned rectangular or square plot; plot width is west–east, depth south–north. Measure the hall offsets from the southwest plot corner. Setbacks use south as front and west as left. The template derives a single hall envelope from supplied panels, viewing depths, aisle widths, end clearances and wall thickness. It checks footprint fit and supplied FSI/coverage limits using the single gross hall footprint; no code-specific area exclusions are inferred.

Enter **1–10 panels per bank**. All panels run south–north, with a usable display face on both long sides after the supplied end margins. The viewing zones are drawn in front of the usable portions. The central aisle, passages between panels, and outer passages are clear widths **in addition to** those zones, so the assumed viewing strips do not consume the through widths. Use zero inter-panel passage for one panel per bank. A centred entry opening in the south wall aligns with and cannot be wider than the central aisle. Clear front and rear strips link the longitudinal paths; no door leaf, exterior approach or second exit is drawn.

Example: on a **25 × 20 m** plot, use two panels per bank, **6 m** long and **0.2 m** thick, with **0.5 m** non-display margins each end and **1.8 m** usable hanging height. With **1.25 m** viewing zones, a **2 m** central aisle, **0.9 m** inter-panel passages, **1.2 m** outer passages, **2 m / 2.5 m** front/rear clear depths and **0.2 m** walls, the hall is **17.4 × 10.9 m**. Its eight faces provide **40 m** of usable display length and **72 m²** of hanging area. These are example inputs, not design criteria.

A fitting layout exports a layered editable DXF in millimetres and a CSV of face IDs, orientation, local coordinates, usable length, height and area. Reopen it from Projects under **Gallery display-panel CAD**. Failed checks preserve the brief but block both exports. This concept does not check occupancy, egress, accessibility, doors, structure, lighting, anchorage, curatorial suitability or mounting height fit.
