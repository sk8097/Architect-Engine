# Measured cricket practice nets CAD

In Project Wizard choose **Institutional → Sports / recreation / public amenities** and Stadium, Sports Complex, Sports Club or Training Centre. Opt into **Cricket: measured practice lanes and crease set-out**. Use a cardinally aligned rectangular or square plot, a blank setting or Outdoor, and an early design or site-development action. Leave building floors, separate Parking and Space selections empty. Football, athletics and other sports modes stay unchanged.

The template fits 1–12 identical south–north lanes side by side. Enter the clear lane width, gap between lanes (zero gives one shared side reference), wicket-to-wicket distance, run-up to the south wicket line, and north clearance behind the batter wicket line. Both flat lengths are checked against submitted minima. The envelope includes side and end reserves. Its offsets and setbacks are measured from the plot edges: west is left and south is front.

Each lane has transverse marks on both wicket lines, popping creases offset infield, and mirrored return creases extending from the popping line toward their own wicket end. For this template the two types of transverse marks use the same submitted width. The paired return-crease separation must be inside the transverse mark width, and that width must be inside the lane. Popping creases cannot meet or cross; the returns must pass the wicket line without extending beyond the lane. The template does not apply regulation crease dimensions or infer net placement.

The **editable DXF** shows lane edges, creases and the reserve envelope in local mm. The **CSV** gives the centre x and wicket/popping y coordinates of every lane in local metres. Both use the southwest plot corner as origin; entered dimensions are rounded to millimetres, but half-mm centres are kept. Failed geometry checks preserve the brief in Projects with a reason, with both exports and generic fallback blocked.

Example: **3 lanes**, each 3.6 m wide with 0.25 m gaps, 20.120 m between wickets, 14 m run-up and 3 m behind the batter. With 1.2 m side and 1 m end reserves, the envelope is **13.700 × 39.120 m**. In the example plot placement the wicket y coordinates are **18.000 m and 38.120 m** and the popping lines are at **19.220 m and 36.900 m**.

This is geometric set-out only, not ball containment, structural support, netting, surface, drainage, access, rules compliance or safety approval.
