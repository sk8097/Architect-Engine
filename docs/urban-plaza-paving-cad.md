# Urban plaza paving grid and cut schedule

Choose **Infrastructure / Public Works → Public realm / landscape / waste → Urban Plaza**, or **Institutional & Public Utility → Sports / recreation / public amenities → Urban Plaza**. Under **Automatic drawing**, select **Urban plaza: centred paving grid and cut schedule**. Set the arrangement to Public Open Space (Infrastructure) or Outdoor (Institutional), or leave it blank. Leave floors, parking and separate Space selections empty. Other drawing modes are unchanged.

Enter a cardinally aligned rectangular or square plot. The paving rectangle is located by its width, depth, and offsets from the west and south plot edges. If you supply setbacks, west is left and south is front. Enter **actual piece dimensions**, not nominal module sizes that already include a joint. Use one positive joint width between all pieces and enter the minimum acceptable installed width and depth. There is no perimeter joint. All dimensions are rounded to millimetres. The straight-bond grid is axis-aligned with no rotations, holes, obstructions or staggered joints.

For each axis, an exact full-piece fit is preferred. Otherwise the grid uses the largest full-piece core that leaves two border pieces within the supplied minimum and full-piece sizes. When the available border width is an odd number of millimetres, the extra millimetre goes east or north. An axis can have only two border pieces if no full core fits. If the centred pattern cannot fit, change the rectangle, piece or joint specification. Plans are limited to 2,500 installed pieces.

**Example:** On a 12 × 8 m plot, start a **7.040 × 4.230 m** rectangle 2 m from west and 1 m from south. Use **600 × 400 mm** pieces with **5 mm** joints and minimum cuts of **200 × 120 mm**. The result is **12 × 11 pieces**, of which **90 are full and 42 are cut**. The west and east border widths are **492 and 493 mm**, and both north and south border depths are **290 mm**. The plan area of joints is **0.5819 m²** out of **29.7792 m²** gross.

Export a layered **DXF in local millimetres** and a **CSV** grouped by installed width and depth, with quantities and net piece areas. Reopen in Projects via **Urban plaza paving CAD**. If a check fails, the brief stays saved with a reason and neither export is available. This is not a purchasing or stock-cutting schedule: it does not account for offcut reuse, saw kerf, waste, spares, edge details, levels, drainage or installation approval.
