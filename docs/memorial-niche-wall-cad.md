# Measured memorial niche wall CAD

Choose **Infrastructure / Public Works → Public realm / landscape / waste → Cemetery/Memorial Landscape**. In **Automatic drawing**, select **Memorial: measured niche walls and elevation**. Choose Public Open Space or Support Facility, or leave the arrangement blank. Leave building floors, parking and separate Space selections empty.

Use a cardinally aligned rectangular or square plot. Plot width runs west–east and depth south–north. A straight west–east row contains **1–6 identical freestanding panels**. For a single face, niches open south. The two-face option repeats the same grid on north and south. Select up to 20 columns and 8 tiers per face. Enter the clear niche width, height and depth, the grid web thickness, a uniform perimeter frame, the solid back or centre spine, and optional plinth height above grade. Overall panel depth is the solid back/spine plus one or two pocket depths. Counts are nominal openings, not a statement about container sizes or usable occupancy.

Use a positive gap for multiple panels or zero for one panel. Measure the **outside layout envelope** from the west and south plot edges. The envelope includes the south viewing strip, the north viewing strip (rear reserve for one face), and reserves at both ends. Setbacks use left = west and front = south. The whole envelope must fit within the submitted setbacks. If supplied, the panel top must also meet the submitted height limit and the total niche count must meet its separate target. Enter zero for no capacity target.

**Example:** Three panels with two faces, eight columns and four tiers give **192 nominal niches**. With **0.36 × 0.32 × 0.40 m** clear niches (width × height × depth), **0.06 m** grid webs, a **0.12 m** frame, **0.16 m** spine and **0.30 m** plinth, each panel is **3.540 m wide × 2.000 m high × 0.960 m deep**. Add **0.50 m** gaps, **1.20 m** at each end, and **1.80 m south / 1.50 m north** viewing strips for a **14.020 × 4.260 m** layout envelope. It fits a **20 × 10 m** plot when offset **2 m from west / 3 m from south**, subject to actual setbacks.

Exports are separate editable layered DXFs in local millimetres: **site plan** with niche pockets shown in projection, and **true-scale typical panel elevation** for the identical face grid. Reopen under **Memorial niche wall CAD** in Projects. Invalid briefs stay saved with a specific reason and no DXF or generic zoning substitute. This is geometry only: no structure, foundations, fastening, weatherproofing, container suitability, access routes or accessibility verification, grading, or cemetery approval.
