# Surveyed open stormwater drain CAD

Choose **Infrastructure / Public Works → Water / wastewater / stormwater → Stormwater Drain** (or **Drainage Network**) → **Drainage drawing → Open stormwater drain: surveyed trapezoidal reach**. This mode is distinct from the buried gravity-pipe layout. Use Drainage System, Network or leave the setting blank; the template is one surveyed reach without separate Space selections.

Record a projected metre grid and source/datum, then exactly two `Easting,Northing,invert` controls, inlet first, separated by `;` or a line break. The two invert levels use the same datum, and the plan length is limited to 2 km. Enter clear bottom width, bank depth above invert, geometric reference depth, each side slope (horizontal run per 1 vertical), maintenance strip width and minimum invert fall. **Left and right are as seen looking downstream**. Enter the available perpendicular corridor distance from the bottom centreline separately for each side; the generator checks that each bank and its strip fits along the entire straight reach. The strip is a width reserve, not a graded road or path.

The reference depth must be below the bank top. The app reports trapezoid area at that reference depth and the remaining vertical distance to bank top. This is **not predicted water level, discharge capacity or operational freeboard**. The design bank and reference lines rise by a constant vertical offset from the invert, not from survey ground.

Example: `500000,2000000,100.200;500030,2000040,99.700` defines a **50 m** reach with **1.000%** downstream grade. With **1.2 m bottom width**, **1.0 m bank depth**, slopes **2H:1V left** and **1.5H:1V right** and a **0.75 m strip**, the top width is **4.7 m** and the required corridor offsets are **3.35 m left** and **2.85 m right**. At **0.6 m reference depth** the area is **1.350 m²** and geometric freeboard is **0.4 m**. These are example inputs, not standards.

The three editable layered DXFs are a projected-mm plan, a true-scale typical section looking downstream, and a true-scale longitudinal profile with local vertical zero at the inlet invert. Reopen from Projects under **Surveyed open drain CAD**. Invalid geometry preserves the brief but blocks all three exports and does not fall back to another drawing. No hydraulic sizing, earthwork, erosion/lining design, bank stability, outlet protection, crossing, survey tie-in or permitting is checked.
