# Surveyed road-crossing culvert CAD

Choose **Infrastructure → Water / wastewater / stormwater → Culvert → Culvert drawing → Surveyed road crossing: culvert plan and barrel profile**. Use a new plan-view Drainage System or Network concept with no floors, parking or separate Space selections.

Supply one common survey grid and level datum. Enter the inlet then outlet as `Easting,Northing,surface,invert`, in projected metres, to three decimal places. The straight barrel centreline and its plan length come from those two points. Optional intervening ground samples are `chainage,level` pairs, measured from the inlet along the barrel; enter them in strictly increasing order, one per line or separated by semicolons. The existing surface is interpolated linearly between samples and end levels.

Enter the station where the barrel crosses the straight road centreline, the road's **grid bearing clockwise from north**, and the width crossed, measured perpendicular to the road. An axis intersection less than 15° from parallel is outside this template. Both full barrel end faces must extend beyond the road band; at a skew crossing their corners can govern. Also enter the outside barrel width in plan, the vertical rise from invert to outside crown, minimum downstream fall and minimum road cover. The minimum cover check uses the road-edge stations and all surveyed breakpoints between them. It does **not** check cover across the barrel width or outside the specified road band.

Example: inlet `345000,2110000,100.200,100.000`, outlet `345030,2110040,99.800,99.500`; intervening samples `10,102.600;20,102.400;30,101.750;40,102.120`. Enter crossing station **25 m**, road bearing **0°**, road width **12 m**, barrel width **2 m** and crown rise **1.2 m**. The **50 m** barrel falls **1%**; roadway centreline cover is measured from station **15 to 35 m** and reaches its minimum of **0.850 m at station 30 m**.

Save to preview and export separate layered **plan and profile DXFs**. Plan coordinates are projected millimetres; profile X is chainage from inlet, and Y is level above the labelled plot datum with the selected vertical scale. Invalid geometry or a failed minimum check preserves the brief but blocks both exports. Reopen **Survey culvert crossing CAD** in Projects. This is not a hydraulic, headwall, geotechnical, utility-clearance or structural assessment.
