# Surveyed separated cycle track and driveway crossings

Choose **Infrastructure / Public Works → Transport infrastructure → Cycle Track**. Under **Corridor drawing**, select **Separated cycle track: surveyed driveway crossings**. Leave separate Space selections empty. The other corridor modes, including the circular-bend template, remain available and unchanged.

In **Site / plot / land**, enter two projected metre-grid controls along a straight road edge (A then B, up to 2 km). Chainage starts at A. Choose the side looking toward B; positive offsets are perpendicular to the road edge on that side. Enter 2–80 ordered `chainage,outward offset` roadside limits, separated by semicolons or line breaks. The first and last samples define the track ends, and limits are connected with straight segments. The track uses constant separator and track widths. The roadside gap is the sample offset minus those two widths; the smallest gap occurs at a survey sample because the limit is piecewise linear. The gap is not the shortest distance to the roadside boundary or a statutory setback.

Enter 1–20 ordered `ID,start chainage,end chainage` driveway windows. Each window stays inside the track extent and crosses perpendicular to the road edge through the separator and track width. The separator centreline breaks at each driveway while the track edges stay continuous. Only the length **between successive driveways** is tested against the submitted minimum separator length; no end reserve is assumed. This does not size the driveway beyond the track.

**Example:** A `(1000,2000)` and B `(1060,2080)`, track on the left. Roadside limits `10,7;40,6;90,8`, separator **1.5 m**, track **3 m**, minimum roadside gap **1.5 m**, and required separator between drives **28 m**. Driveways `D1,20,26;D2,54,62` leave **66 m** of separator over the **80 m** track, and overlap **42 m²** of track surface. The governing roadside gap is **1.5 m at Ch 40 m**.

Export an editable layered **DXF in projected millimetres** and a driveway **CSV** with chainages, span along the track and track overlap area. Reopen from Projects using **Surveyed cycle track CAD**. Failed checks retain the brief with a specific reason and block both exports without a generic fallback. Horizontal geometry only: no sight distance, vehicle turning, markings, priority, longitudinal grades, drainage, accessibility or traffic safety approval.
