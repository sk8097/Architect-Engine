# Public garden raised-bed CAD

Choose **Infrastructure / Public Works → Public realm / landscape / waste → Public Garden → Automatic drawing → Public garden: raised beds, paths and medium takeoff**. Use Public Open Space or no arrangement, with no above-grade floors, parking or separate Space selections.

Enter a cardinally aligned rectangular or square plot. Plot width is west–east; depth is south–north. For offsets use the southwest plot corner; setbacks use south as front and west as left.

Enter **1–30 identical bed pairs** (one west and one east of the central path, numbered from south to north). The outside wall rectangles are surrounded by a central path, side paths and end paths. With several pairs, each clear cross-aisle joins the central and side paths. Use zero cross-aisle depth for one pair. All paths are internal clear plan areas; there is no approach from the plot boundary.

The clear growing dimensions subtract two wall thicknesses from each outside dimension. The centreline must be reachable within the supplied horizontal reach measured from either long **outside** edge; this includes the width of the wall. Fill must not exceed the usable inside wall height. The takeoff multiplies clear growing area by fill depth, without allowance for compaction, waste or settlement. Internal path area excludes the entire outer bed footprints.

Example: a **12 × 22 m** plot and four pairs of **1.4 × 2.6 m** beds with **0.10 m** walls, **0.35 m** fill and **0.70 m** reach. A **1.8 m** central path, **1.2 m** side paths / cross-aisles and **1.5 m** end paths yield a **7 × 17 m** envelope at west/south offsets of **2 m / 3 m**. The eight beds have **23.040 m²** of growing area and need **8.064 m³** of net growing medium. The internal paths cover **89.880 m²**. These are example inputs, not accessibility criteria.

A fitting layout exports a layered editable DXF in local millimetres and a CSV with bed IDs, southwest coordinates, outside/inside dimensions, fill depth, growing area and medium volume. Reopen under **Public garden raised-bed CAD** in Projects. Failed geometry preserves the brief and blocks both exports. The template does not check grades, surface material, accessibility, wall/foundation design, irrigation or drainage.
