# Measured silage bunker CAD

Choose **Rural / Agricultural → Farm / farmhouse / agricultural estate → Dairy Farm or Cattle Farm → Farm: measured silage bunker and geometric fill**. Use a cardinally aligned rectangular or square plot, a blank arrangement or Production Site, and an early design or site-development action. Leave floors, separate Parking and Space selections empty. The cattle-shed template stays separate.

The template draws one open-front rectangular bunker. The south threshold and apron are at zero elevation. The floor rises linearly to the rear, while the fill face rises from zero at the opening to a level crest. The crest must begin before the rear wall and be higher than the rear floor. The wall top is level; wall top minus crest is the vertical freeboard. Floor grade is rear rise divided by clear length, and fill-face grade is crest elevation divided by horizontal face run. Both are checked against independently supplied maximum percentages.

For width `W`, clear length `L`, rear floor rise `R`, fill crest elevation `F` and horizontal face run `A`, the geometric volume is `W × (L×F − L×R/2 − A×F/2)`. This subtracts the rising floor and the open-front wedge. The net geometry must meet the submitted minimum volume; no density or packing factor is assumed.

The fitting envelope includes side and rear walls, side and rear service reserves and the full-width south apron. Offsets locate this envelope, not the clear bunker. Setbacks use west as left and south as front. Lengths round to mm, with grade and volume checked before display rounding.

The **editable plan DXF** shows the open-front shape, apron and crest start. The **true-scale longitudinal section DXF** shows the floor and the full fill profile. Local units are mm. Conflicts leave the brief accessible in Projects but block both exports and generic fallback.

Example: an **8 × 24 m** clear bunker, **0.480 m** rear floor rise, **2.400 m** crest and **6 m** fill-face run yield **357.120 m³** of geometric fill, with **2.000%** floor grade and **40.000%** face grade. A **3.000 m** wall top leaves **0.600 m** freeboard. With 0.25 m walls, 1.25 m reserves and a 6 m apron, the full envelope is **11.000 × 31.500 m**.

This is geometry only. It does not check storage mass, density, compaction, pile stability, access, leachate, drainage, structure, fire or operational compliance.
