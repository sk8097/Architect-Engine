# Open-air public market CAD

Choose **Institutional & Public Utility → Sports / recreation / public amenities → Public Market → Automatic drawing → Open-air public market: measured stalls and circulation**. Use Outdoor or leave the setting blank; no above-grade floors, parking or separate Space selections are used.

Use a rectangular or square plot with north, east, south or west frontage. Measure plot width parallel to the chosen frontage and depth inward. Left and right are as seen from the frontage, looking into the plot. The market offset locates its circulation envelope relative to those plot edges.

Enter **1–24 stalls per row**, stall frontage (measured along the central aisle), depth, and centred opening width. The two rows face each other. Stall openings are drawn as real gaps in the DXF and can span the full stall frontage. The shopping aisle connects both cross-aisles, which in turn connect the passages behind both stall rows. The centred approach runs from the frontage to the front cross-aisle; its width cannot exceed the shopping aisle. The plot boundary is a parcel reference, not a fence. Setbacks apply to the market envelope; the approach intentionally crosses the front setback. The internal circulation area excludes this approach and subtracts both stall rows from the market envelope.

Example: on a **20 × 30 m** plot, place the market envelope **2 m** from the left and **4 m** from the frontage. Use **five 3 × 2.4 m stalls per row**, **1.8 m openings**, a **3.6 m aisle**, **1.8 m side passages**, **2.5 m front** and **3 m rear cross-aisles**, and a **2.4 m approach**. The result is a **12 × 20.5 m** envelope, with **72 m²** for the ten stalls and **174 m²** of internal circulation. These are example inputs, not minimum widths or regulations.

The valid preview exports a layered editable DXF in millimetres and can be reopened from Projects. Failed geometry keeps the measured brief, with a reason and no fallback drawing. This concept does not verify occupancy, emergency egress, accessibility, toilets, drainage, stall structures or vehicle/service access.
