# Measured dock leveller CAD

In the Project Wizard choose **Industrial → Warehouse / logistics → General Warehouse or Distribution Centre** and **Warehouse: measured dock leveller and truck-bed height range** under Automatic drawing. Use a blank arrangement, Open Storage or Rack Storage and an early design or site-development action. Use a cardinal rectangle or square, at most one floor, and no separate parking or Space selections.

Model one south-facing bay with a stationary centred truck. Enter the hinge height and the minimum and maximum truck-bed heights above a common datum. The main deck has a fixed hinge-to-joint length; an articulated lip is idealized as horizontal at the truck-bed elevation. The gap from the dock hinge to the truck rear edge stays fixed across the height range. Enter the clear centred landing width and usable landing depth measured from that rear edge, the deck/lip width, and the minimum joint clearance, overlap and maximum grade you want to check. The north-side dock reserve, both service-side reserves and full usable landing strip count in the plot fit. Locate the southwest corner of that envelope; west is left and south is front for setbacks.

For deck length `L` and height difference `Δ`, horizontal run is `sqrt(L² − Δ²)` and grade magnitude is `100 × abs(Δ) / run`. The endpoint farther from the hinge gives the steepest grade and shortest overlap. The height closest to the hinge gives the longest reach (the level position when inside the range). That interior position can govern minimum clearance between the joint and rear edge and maximum landing depth, even if both endpoints fit. Overlap is `run + lipLength − rearEdgeDistance`; clearance is `rearEdgeDistance − run`. Linear inputs normalize to millimetres before checking.

The editable local-mm plan DXF shows the full envelope, landing strip, maximum deck/lip projection, shortest lip tip and A–A cut. A separate true-scale section shows both submitted height limits and the longest-reach position if distinct. Section x starts at the hinge and grows south. The saved project's **Dock leveller CAD** action reopens the sheets; a failed brief reopens with the reason and without DXF or generic zoning fallback.

**Check case:** Hinge at 1.200 m, bed range 0.500–1.700 m, a 2.500 m deck, 0.450 m lip and 2.550 m hinge-to-rear distance give **0.300–0.400 m** overlap, **0.050 m** minimum joint clearance and **29.167%** peak grade. A 0.399 m landing fails at the interior level position, despite fitting at both height endpoints. With a 2.400 m landing width, 0.800 m side reserves and a 1.500 m dock reserve, the full envelope is **4.000 × 4.450 m**.

This is static, zero-thickness geometry only. It does not select leveller equipment, model lip deployment or vehicle motion, check vehicle or under-deck clearances, verify loads, anchors or slip resistance, or establish operating or safety approval.
