# Primary school arrival and learning CAD

**New Project → Institutional & Public Utility → Education → Primary School → Automatic drawing → Primary school: visitor lobby and classroom route**. The scope is a new single-storey, single-building rectangular or square school on a measured plot with cardinal frontage. Enter wall, passage, door and building dimensions, offsets and a lobby depth (from the inside of the front wall to the internal door). Optionally enter a clear width for a straight on-site walk from the frontage to the main door; this ends at the property boundary, not in the street.

Include one measured administration/check-in room, one or more classrooms and student toilets. Enter the clear classroom dimensions, quantity and an exterior window width; reconcile the classroom quantities with Facility requirements. Add only the listed optional support rooms. The administration room sits on the front edge and opens into the lobby. Other room doors must open wholly beyond the internal threshold into the learning passage. App-generated walls, door swings and room clear sizes remain editable. Required site and room fit checks, including conflicting relationships and offsets, block the export rather than switching to generic zoning.

Save generates a concept automatically and preserves the brief if the fit fails. Reopen from **Projects → Primary school CAD**. DXF layers include `SCHOOL_ARRIVAL`, `SCHOOL_LEARNING`, `SCHOOL_THRESHOLD` and, when specified, `SCHOOL_ENTRY_WALK`. The threshold is a physical interior door and a supervision planning point, not a security-device or lock design. This is not an evacuation plan: independently verify additional exits, door hardware, occupancy and travel distances, accessibility, daylight and ventilation, supervision, and local education/building rules. Drop-off, parking, laboratories, halls, campuses and multi-floor schools are not part of this output.

## Optional rear playground

In **Spaces**, select **Playground** once and supply its clear width and depth in Details. In **Site**, confirm permission for rear open play and fence siting, and enter the rear door width, walk width and length, fence thickness and gate clear width. The app places the yard behind the school, centered on the student passage. It cuts a rear door in the plan, draws a straight site walk to one gate, and outlines the full measured fence around the clear play area. Conflicting requirements or any part extending outside the plot block the whole drawing; the app does not silently omit the playground.

The playground, walk/door/gate, and fence use `SCHOOL_PLAYGROUND`, `SCHOOL_PLAY_WALK` and `SCHOOL_PLAY_FENCE` DXF layers. This is not an emergency exit, a route to the street, a fence/security specification or a playground equipment/clearance design. Review independently for supervision, accessible grades and surfaces, drainage, structural/edge safety and fire evacuation.
