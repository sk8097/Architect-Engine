# Outdoor amphitheatre CAD

Choose **Institutional & Public Utility → Culture / worship / community → Theatre** (or **Auditorium** / **Performing Arts Centre**) → **Automatic drawing → Outdoor amphitheatre: stage, tiers and centreline sightline**. Leave the indoor hall arrangement, building floors, parking and separate Space selections empty.

Use a cardinally aligned rectangular or square plot. Width is west–east and depth is south–north. The stage sits south of the seating, both centred on a common axis. Measure offsets from the southwest plot corner. Setbacks use south as front and west as left. The envelope includes the stage, gap to the seating, stepped bank and side/rear plan reserves. The reserves do not represent designed paths or stairs.

Supply **2–28 straight rows**, uniform tread/pitch and rise, clear bank width, margin at each end and nominal place width. The count is the number of full place widths between the two margins, up to 80 per row, with any leftover width split at the ends. Individual plan marks are nominal places, not an approved occupancy count.

The true-scale section puts the first tread at elevation zero and each representative eye at mid-tread, using the supplied eye height above that tread. Each ray is aimed at the **centre of the audience-facing stage deck edge**, not the entire stage. The app checks that the rays do not pass below the tread fronts and computes each row's vertical clearance above the preceding row's eye. The minimum is compared with the supplied criterion. The DXF highlights the controlling ray and clearance. This is a single-axis proxy, not a check of full-stage visibility, heads, railings or other obstructions.

Example: on a **24 × 24 m** plot, use six **0.90 m** treads and **0.35 m** rises, a **12 m** seating bank with **0.25 m** end margins and **0.50 m** nominal place width. A **12 × 5 m** stage at **0.65 m**, with a **3 m** gap and **1.2 m** eye height, gives **138 nominal places** and a minimum centreline clearance of **about 89.62 mm** at row 6. With **2 m** side and rear reserves the envelope is **16 × 15.4 m**. These are example inputs, not design criteria.

Save to preview and export separate editable layered DXFs for the plan and section. Reopen from Projects under **Outdoor amphitheatre CAD**. A failed fit or clearance preserves the brief with a reason and blocks export. No structure, crowd movement, egress, accessibility, guarding, acoustic performance or occupancy compliance is checked.
