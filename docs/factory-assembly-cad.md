# Dry factory assembly CAD

**Industrial & Warehousing → Manufacturing / factory → Assembly Plant, Light Manufacturing or Electronics Assembly → Automatic drawing → Factory assembly: material route, staff passage and benches**. Confirm dry non-hazardous work, separate staff and receiving access on the frontage, and dispatch access at the rear. Use one new ground-floor building, a measured rectangular or square plot, and the Assembly Line arrangement or leave the arrangement unspecified.

Select one Raw Material Receiving, Production Line, Quality Control and Dispatch room. Incoming Inspection, Raw Store and Finished Goods are optional single rooms. Enter each clear depth and the full process-room width (gross shell width minus three wall thicknesses and the clear staff passage). The total clear depths plus the exterior and transverse walls must equal the measured shell depth. Room order follows the material sequence, not the order in which the spaces are selected.

A walled side passage runs from a separate front staff entrance to controlled doorway positions at Production Line and Quality Control. People and goods do not use the same exterior opening. The material lane runs straight through each processing stage between the measured front and rear pads. In production, the work aisle meets the staff door and directly adjoins all equal bench modules. The working clearance around each bench is drawn separately from the furniture and cannot overlap the material lane.

Save generates the preview from the brief. Reopen **Factory assembly CAD** from the project menu. The editable DXF includes `ASSEMBLY_OPENINGS`, `ASSEMBLY_MATERIAL`, `ASSEMBLY_PADS`, `ASSEMBLY_STAFF`, `ASSEMBLY_WORK_AISLE`, `ASSEMBLY_BENCHES` and `ASSEMBLY_CLEARANCE`. Failed fit keeps the brief but blocks export; the app does not drop machines, reduce dimensions or substitute generic zoning.

This is concept geometry, not machine or factory safety design. No hazardous processes, cleanroom envelopes, guarding, lift/vehicle turning, utilities, floor loads, ergonomics, exhaust, fire/egress, rated separation or approval compliance are verified. Exterior access beyond the measured pads is not designed.
