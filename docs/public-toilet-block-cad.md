# Measured public toilet block CAD

Choose **Institutional & Public Utility → Sports / recreation / public amenities → Public Toilet Block**, or **Infrastructure / Public Works → Public realm / landscape / waste → Public Toilet**. Under **Automatic drawing**, select **Public toilet: measured rooms and clear entry**. Use one ground floor with no separate Parking or Space selections. For the institutional type select Indoor or Indoor + Outdoor; for the infrastructure type select Support Facility or Public Open Space. Leaving the arrangement blank also works.

Use a cardinally aligned rectangular or square plot: width west–east, depth south–north. Setbacks use west as left and south as front. Offsets locate the southwest corner of the whole block and shared south entrance gallery. The combined envelope must fit the supplied setbacks, including the gallery. The plan does not establish a route from the gallery to a site boundary.

Enter **1–6 identical single-occupancy rooms**, one row with uniform walls and centred clear openings to the gallery. For each of the two fixture envelopes (WC and basin), enter its southwest-corner offsets from the **inside** southwest corner of the room, then its width and depth. Fixture rectangles must not overlap and must fit inside the clear room. Enter the diameter and south-wall offset of the centre of a supplied clear manoeuvring circle. It is centred across the room width. A straight clear strip, no wider than the doorway, continues from the gallery through the wall opening to the circle centre. Both reserves are checked against each fixture; the circle uses true nearest-point distance rather than its enclosing square. Boundary contact is geometrically allowed, not a recommended construction tolerance.

**Geometry example:** On a **16 × 12 m** plot, three **3.20 × 3.50 m** rooms, **0.20 m** walls and a **1.80 m** gallery make a **10.400 × 5.700 m** envelope. Use a **0.95 m** opening, a **0.90 m** clear entry strip and a **1.60 m** circle centred **1.70 m** from the inside south wall. WC example: origin **(0.25, 2.65) m** and **0.70 × 0.75 m** footprint. Basin: origin **(2.40, 1.10) m** and **0.60 × 0.60 m** footprint. At **2 m** offsets from both west and south plot edges, there is **3.60 m east** and **4.30 m north** for the supplied setbacks. These are sample dimensions, not accessibility standards.

The editable layered DXF separates walls, the gallery, openings, fixture footprints, entry strips and native arc clearance guides, in local millimetres. Reopen under **Public toilet block CAD** in Projects. If checks fail, the brief stays saved with a specific reason, without DXF or a generic zoning substitute. This mode does not check door leaves or swings, transfer/fixture reach, grab bars, construction tolerances, privacy, plumbing, ventilation or accessibility compliance.
