# Paired 1 BHK apartment CAD

Choose **Residential → Apartment / multi-family housing → 1 BHK, Rental Apartment, Apartment building or Low-Rise Apartment**. In Configuration, choose **Automatic drawing → Paired 1 BHK flats: shared lobby and mirrored rooms**. Enter a measured rectangle or square, cardinal frontage, and **one ground floor**, with **Low-Rise** or **Walk-Up** form if specified. Enter **2 total units** and **1 BHK** for the unit mix. Leave separate Space selections empty; this mode draws the full specified pair directly.

Enter envelope and wall widths, shared door width, offsets, lobby dimensions and the front approach. Each flat enters off the common lobby and has a front living/kitchen room and separate rear bedroom and bathroom accessed directly from living. There is no internal opening between the two flats. The bathrooms back onto a common service-space reserve entered separately from the lobby; the app does not design services or sanitary fixtures. The counter and working clearance mirror from side to side. Living and bedroom windows are sized from the brief.

A **15.4 × 9 m** envelope on an **85 × 75 ft** plot with 3 m left and 4 m front offsets can fit two 6.2 m wide flats with a 2.2 × 2.8 m lobby and 1.6 m approach. Use 0.2 m walls, 1 m doors, 5.2 m living depths, 2.1 m bathroom widths, a 3.4 × 0.65 m kitchen run with 1.2 m working clearance, and 2.4 m living / 1.8 m bedroom windows. The examples are dimensional inputs, not standards.

**Save** generates the preview and editable DXF from the entered measurements. If the room, opening, kitchen, lobby or setback geometry conflicts, the brief stays saved but export is blocked. Reopen from **Paired apartment CAD** in Projects.

No lift, separate kitchen, parking, terrace, balcony, additional units, plumbing, accessibility, acoustic/fire rating, daylight compliance or construction design is included.
