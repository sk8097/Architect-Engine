# Surveyed footbridge crossing CAD

Choose **Infrastructure / Public Works → Transport infrastructure → Bridge → Bridge drawing → Surveyed footbridge: straight deck and approaches over a channel**. Use Linear Corridor, Node / Interchange or leave the arrangement blank. Do not add separate Space selections.

Enter the projected metre grid and vertical datum, and exactly two `Easting,Northing,tie-in level` rows, A then B, separated by `;` or a line break. These are the outside ends of the approaches; their straight plan alignment (up to 2 km long) defines the chainage from A. Record the channel centreline crossing chainage, its grid bearing clockwise from north, the perpendicular channel width and the required perpendicular bank gap for the deck ends. The channel axis must be at least 15° from parallel to the bridge.

Supply the start and end deck chainages, full plan width, level deck top and vertical depth to its underside. The section uses one straight grade between A and the deck and one between the deck and B, with a level deck in between. The checker uses both **full-width deck end faces** against the skewed banks, not only the centreline. Magnitudes of both approach grades must meet the supplied maximum. Using a single supplied reference water elevation, the checker subtracts it from the deck underside and compares the result against the supplied minimum; it does not predict flood levels.

Example: `500000,2000000,100;500080,2000000,100.2` gives **80 m** between tie-ins. Enter a **20 m** channel at bearing **30°**, crossing at chainage **40 m**, with **1 m** minimum bank gap and **3 m** deck width. The full-width required range is approximately **26.432–53.568 m**, so a deck from **26–54 m** fits. With deck RL **101.3 m**, depth **0.5 m** and reference water RL **99.4 m**, the geometrical clearance is **1.4 m**; approach grades are **5.000% / 4.231%**. These are example inputs, not dimensional standards.

Valid geometry exports separate editable layered DXFs: a projected-mm plan and a true-scale centreline profile in local millimetres. Reopen under **Surveyed footbridge CAD** from Projects. Failed checks preserve the brief but block both exports. This concept does not assess structure, deck loading, abutments, foundations, hydraulics, flood risk, surveyed approach surfaces, landings, guarding, accessibility or authority approval.
