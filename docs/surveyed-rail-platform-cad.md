# Surveyed side-platform CAD

Choose **Infrastructure / Public Works → Transport infrastructure → Railway Station** or **Metro Station**. In **Rail platform drawing**, choose **Surveyed side platform: track offset and cross-section**. This is a survey-only mode; leave Space selections empty. It does not use a building footprint, floors or parking. Select Node / Interchange, Terminal / Depot or Linear Corridor, or leave arrangement blank. The ordinary station and bicycle modes are unchanged unless this survey mode is selected.

In **Site**, enter the projected metre grid, a common elevation datum and the reference rail. Supply two projected Easting,Northing controls A and B for a straight track centreline (no geographic degrees). Looking **from A toward B**, choose left or right. Chainage starts at A and increases along the line. Enter 2–60 ordered `chainage,reference rail level,platform edge level` samples; the **first and last samples are the actual platform ends**. Separate records by semicolons or newlines. Survey input uses up to three decimal places. The model linearly interpolates edge and reference levels between samples.

Enter the actual perpendicular offset of the track-side platform edge and an independent submitted minimum static offset. The clear width is total width minus the track-side strip and optional back reserve. Neither strip specifies a finished tactile system. Supply the minimum and maximum **signed difference** `edge level − rail reference level` and the maximum absolute longitudinal edge grade. The app checks the signed band at each sample and grade on each intervening straight segment. The transverse section is at the **smallest signed difference** (first occurrence if tied). Its vertical zero is the reference rail RL at that station; horizontal distance increases from track centreline toward the platform. The platform surface falls away from the track at the submitted crossfall across the whole width.

**Geometry example, not design standard:** A `(500000,2000000)` and B `(500096,2000128)` are 160 m apart. Use left side, 1.80 m offset, 6.00 m width, 0.80 m track-side strip, 1.00 m back reserve, and 2% crossfall. Samples `20,100.000,100.950; 60,100.200,101.100; 120,100.400,101.400` give a **100 m** platform, **4.20 m** clear band, **0.900–1.000 m** signed differences and maximum **0.500%** edge grade. The section is at **Ch 60 m**.

Save to preview and export two editable layered DXFs: the plan in projected millimetres, and the true-scale transverse section in local millimetres. Reopen under **Surveyed rail platform CAD** in Projects. Failed checks keep the brief but block both exports and generic fallback. There is no track gauge/cant, curvature, train swept envelope, boarding gap/step or rolling stock interface, structure, drainage, tactile design, access/egress or railway approval check.
