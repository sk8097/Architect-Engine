# Measured outdoor switchback stair CAD

Choose **Infrastructure / Public Works → Public realm / landscape / waste → Public Park** or **Urban Plaza**. Under **Automatic drawing**, select **Public space: measured switchback stair**. Leave the arrangement blank or select Public Open Space, and leave floors, parking and separate Space selections empty. This is an open-air stair, separate from the switchback ramp mode.

Enter lower and upper landing levels in one datum. The west flight rises north to a full-width turn landing, then the east flight rises south to a separate upper landing. The flights sit beside each other, with the submitted separator reserve between them. Both south landings have equal depth. This template requires both south and turn landing depths to be at least the clear flight width. The separator is a plan reserve, not a guard detail.

The app uses the fewest equal risers per flight that keep the riser at or below the submitted maximum (`ceil(total rise ÷ (2 × maximum riser))`, at least two). If that count gives a riser below the minimum or exceeds 24 per flight, the template cannot fit. Each flight has `risers − 1` horizontal goings. Exact fractional riser heights are retained up to export precision; the turn lands halfway between the entered levels. The footprint is `2 × flight width + separator` by `south landing depth + flight run + turn landing depth`. Locate the full envelope from the west and south plot edges. Setbacks use west as left and south as front.

**Example:** Lower RL **100.000 m**, upper RL **103.750 m**, risers allowed **140–170 mm**, and **290 mm** going. The result is **12 risers per flight at 156.250 mm**, **11 goings** and a **3.190 m** run. Turn landing RL is **101.875 m**. With a **1.50 m** flight, **0.30 m** separator, **1.60 m** south landings and **1.80 m** turn, the full envelope is **3.300 × 6.590 m**. These are geometric example inputs, not design standards.

Export separate editable layered DXFs for the **plan** and **true-scale side elevation**. The elevation superposes the two parallel flight surfaces in the north–south direction; it is not a section through a single flight. Reopen in Projects → **Outdoor switchback stair CAD**. A failed check saves the brief and reason but blocks both exports without substituting generic CAD. The template does not check slope of existing ground, guards, handrails, nosings, surface drainage, structure, headroom, accessible routes or egress approval.
