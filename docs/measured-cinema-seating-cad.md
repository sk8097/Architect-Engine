# Measured cinema seating CAD

Choose **Commercial → Business / service facilities → Cinema / Entertainment**. Under **Automatic drawing**, select **Cinema: measured seat banks and rear flexible bays**. Leave Service zoning blank or select Public-Facing. Use one ground floor (the floor count may be blank), with no separate Space selections or Parking. This is a single clear seating hall, not a complete cinema or multiplex plan.

Enter the clear hall width, the position of its southwest corner on the rectangular/square plot, and the widths of the side and centre aisles. The two identical banks share the space left over after these aisles. Each bank fits as many whole seat modules as possible up to the submitted per-bank maximum. Rows are centred in their banks. `row pitch − occupied seat depth` is the **clear strip behind occupied seats**, including behind the last fixed row before the rear flexible bays. Fixed-seat counts alone must meet the submitted target.

The rear row has identical measured flexible bays centred in each bank, with a full-width rear cross aisle immediately behind them. Their count is reported separately; the bays are not certified accessible spaces. The centred screen line is south of all seating, with distance to the first seat fronts measured horizontally. Hall depth equals front distance to the first row plus all fixed row pitches, rear bay depth and rear aisle depth. West is left and south is front for setbacks. The checked rectangle is the **clear hall**, so add shell, lobbies and other reserves separately outside this tool.

**Example:** A **14.0 m** clear hall with **1.2 m** side aisles and **1.6 m** centre aisle leaves **5 m per bank**. Eight rows at **1.05 m** pitch with **0.55 × 0.60 m** occupied modules fit **144 fixed seats** (9 per bank per row), leaving a **0.450 m** clear strip behind occupied seats. Three **1.10 × 1.50 m** flexible bays per bank add **six flexible bays**, not fixed seats. A first-row offset of **4.0 m**, a screen offset of **0.5 m**, and a **1.4 m** rear cross aisle give a **3.5 m** screen-to-first-row gap and a **15.3 m** clear hall depth. These are model inputs, not construction or occupancy standards.

Export a layered **DXF in local millimetres** and a **row schedule CSV** that separates fixed seats and flexible bays. Reopen through Projects → **Cinema seating CAD**. A failed check saves the brief with a reason but blocks both exports without switching to generic CAD. The tool does not assess doors, floor rake, sightlines, acoustics, egress, accessibility or approved occupant load.
