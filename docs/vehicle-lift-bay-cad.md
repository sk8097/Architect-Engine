# Measured vehicle service bay CAD

Choose **Industrial & Warehousing → Workshop → Vehicle Repair**, **Equipment Maintenance** or **Mechanical Workshop**. Under **Automatic drawing**, select **Workshop: measured vehicle lift bays and section**. Use one ground floor, no separate Parking or Space selections, and enter **1–4 Service bays** plus **Required clear height**. Use the lowest unobstructed height above that floor. If a Single or Multiple Service Bay arrangement is selected, its count must match.

Use a cardinally aligned rectangular or square plot. Width runs west–east and depth south–north. Setbacks use west as left and south as front. The offsets locate the southwest corner of the full workshop and apron layout. Identical bays are side by side without internal walls. The doors face a full-width south apron. Near and far vehicle reserves, plus vehicle length and perimeter wall thickness, set the hall depth. The apron and hall together must fit inside the supplied setbacks; no route is added to a plot boundary.

Each bay centres the vehicle, clear doorway and two matching lift posts laterally. The posts are drawn centred halfway along the vehicle envelope. The clear opening must fit below the hall’s clear height, leave side jambs, and fit the floor-level vehicle with its supplied side and head margins. The post gap must fit the same side margins, and the outer faces of the posts must leave their own requested per-side reserve within the bay. The geometry does not model door hardware or any approach or lift movement.

The true-scale transverse section at the posts shows the rectangular vehicle envelope at the submitted raised base height. The roof is `raised base height + vehicle height`. The governing overhead gap is `clear height − max(raised vehicle roof, post top)`, checked against the supplied minimum. Neither the shown base height nor the visible space below it verifies permissible lift stroke or working clearance.

**Example:** Two **4.80 m** bays with **0.20 m** perimeter walls, a **5.20 m** vehicle and **1.60 / 1.40 m** front/rear reserves give a **10.000 × 8.600 m** hall. A **4 m** apron gives a **10.000 × 12.600 m** overall envelope. On an **18 × 20 m** plot offset **2.50 m west-edge to layout / 3.00 m south-edge to layout**, this leaves **5.50 m east / 4.40 m north**. With a **1.95 m** vehicle on a **1.80 m** raised base, roof level is **3.750 m**; **4.000 m** posts govern clearance below the **4.600 m** clear height, leaving **0.600 m** above them. These are example inputs, not installation recommendations.

Export separate editable layered DXFs for the **workshop plan** and **transverse section**, in local millimetres. Reopen under **Vehicle service bay CAD** in Projects. A failed check keeps the brief, reports the reason and blocks both exports instead of falling back to generic zoning. No lift arms or moving parts, load or anchorage design, driving/turning checks, working-under-vehicle clearance, exhaust/fire services or code approval are provided.
