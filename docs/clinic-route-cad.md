# Separate patient and staff/service routes for outpatient clinics

Use **New Project → Institutional & Public Utility → Healthcare → General Clinic** (or another supported outpatient type). In **Automatic drawing**, choose **Outpatient clinic: separate patient and service routes**. Provide one rectangular ground-floor envelope and setbacks, patient wing width, both passage widths, entrance orientation and measured wall/door/room dimensions. Assign each space to the patient or service route. The service wing uses the remaining width; entrances can use different positions on the same frontage or opposite sides of the building.

This template requires a patient waiting room and a consultation/examination room, plus a measured staff facility/changing room or store. These requirements describe the template, not statutory clinical minima. Requested adjoining rooms cannot fall on opposite sides of the solid divider. All doors are checked within their assigned wing. Each route has its own DXF layer. Requested windows must be on the outside envelope, not the divider. If a drawing is blocked, the project retains the supplied inputs and no alternate zoning plan is automatically exported.

The two wings have **no internal transfer**. Use the template only when the brief explicitly accepts separate external access. The drawing does not plan exterior walkways, secure access control, emergency egress, accessibility, infection control, sterile/dirty supplies, medical gas, or clinical licensing. It does not support inpatient wards, operating theatres, ambulance routes, emergency or other specialist layouts. It is an outpatient planning concept, not a hospital design or an approval drawing.
