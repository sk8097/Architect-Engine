# Measured outdoor basketball court CAD

Choose **Institutional & Public Utility → Sports / recreation / public amenities → Sports Complex, Sports Club, Training Centre, Playground or Recreation Centre → Automatic drawing → Outdoor basketball: measured markings and run-offs**. Select **Outdoor**, a measured rectangular or square plot, and cardinal frontage. Leave floors, parking and separate Space selections empty.

Enter the marked width and baseline-to-baseline length, clear run-off on each side and end, offsets of the run-off envelope from the left and front plot edges, and a clear approach width. The approach starts at the plot frontage and joins the front run-off. The run-off envelope is checked against measured plot edges and supplied setbacks; the approach may cross the front setback.

Enter the basket centre offset, centre-circle radius, key width and depth, free-throw semicircle radius, three-point radius and corner-line inset from the sideline. All dimensions locate marking **centrelines**. The app computes where the straight corner lines intersect the three-point arcs, mirrors each end and checks that the key, free-throw circle and centre circle do not clash. These are supplied dimensions, not a league preset.

Example: on a **26 × 44 m** plot, a **15 × 28 m** court with **2 m** side and **2.5 m** end run-offs gives a **19 × 33 m** clear envelope. Offset it **3 m** left and **4 m** from the front; enter a **1.8 m** approach. Use **1.575 m** basket offset, **1.8 m** centre and free-throw radii, **4.9 × 5.8 m** keys, **6.75 m** three-point radius and **0.9 m** corner inset. Native CAD arcs meet the straight corner lines at both ends.

Save to preview and share a layered editable **DXF** in local millimetres. Reopen **Outdoor basketball court CAD** in Projects. On failed fit the brief stays saved and export is blocked. The drawing does not include equipment, line thickness, restricted-area or auxiliary markings, access grades, drainage, safety verification or league certification.
