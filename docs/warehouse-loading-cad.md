# Warehouse loading CAD

In New Project, choose **Industrial & Warehousing → Warehouse / logistics**, then a supported type: General Warehouse, Distribution Centre, Fulfilment Centre, E-Commerce Warehouse, Bulk Storage or Agricultural Warehouse. Under **Automatic drawing**, choose **Warehouse loading and staff routes**. It is a single-ground-floor, single-sided loading model, not a cross-dock or a specialist storage/automation generator.

- Enter the surveyed rectangular/square site, vehicle frontage, and applicable measured setbacks. Supply shell dimensions and wall thickness, dock count and each bay/opening width, approach and bay depths, staging depth, staff path/door dimensions, and the required apron reserve from the project's vehicle basis. Dimensions are in metres and are converted to integer millimetres for geometry. The storage depth is the shell clear depth remaining after staging.
- The planner places a linked vehicle approach, manoeuvring reserve and measured bays, a loading opening at each bay, and an internal staging and storage area. A different on-plot path reaches a staff swing door and marked staff aisle. Containment, partition coverage and connections are checked. The paths begin at the measured frontage; the generator does not validate permission or external access there.
- Save to preview and export an editable DXF. Vehicle, pedestrian, bay and opening entities are separated into layers. A failed specialised plan shows an explanation, not a generic zoning fallback. Reopen from the project's menu.

The apron is a **rectangle with the specified minimum clear width and depth**, not a calculated vehicle path. Pedestrian approach footprints do not cross vehicle footprints in this model, but the internal aisle is only a marking. Swept path, roadway access, pedestrians/vehicles at interfaces, traffic barriers, gradients, dock equipment/levels, crane/rack clearances, emergency access and statutory review still need verification. Parking, cross-dock, weighbridge, canopy, gatehouse, and trailer parking are not drawn. Do not use it for construction.
