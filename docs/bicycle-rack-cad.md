# Measured bicycle rack CAD

Choose **Institutional & Public Utility → Sports / recreation / public amenities → Public Parking**, or **Infrastructure / Public Works → Transport infrastructure → Railway Station, Metro Station or Transit Interchange**. Under **Automatic drawing**, choose **Bicycle parking: measured two-sided stands**. Use Outdoor (Public Parking), Node / Interchange or Terminal / Depot (stations), or leave arrangement blank. Leave above-grade floors, separate Parking and Space selections empty. This mode draws just the bicycle parking, not the wider station or vehicle park.

Use a cardinally aligned rectangular or square plot, with width west–east and depth south–north. Use west as left and south as front for setbacks. Measure the offset to the **outside edge of the full layout**, including outer reserves and end head spaces. The aisle runs north–south. The west row always exists; the optional second row mirrors it east of the aisle. A single row also has an east outer reserve beyond its aisle.

Each stand has two static bicycle envelopes, one on either side of the stand along the row. Enter the parked-cycle envelope width and length, the stand footprint and the flank gap from stand edge to each loaded bicycle. The occupied length along the aisle for each loaded stand is `2 × (bicycle width + flank gap) + stand width`. Centre-to-centre pitch must cover that span plus the supplied minimum gap to the next loaded stand. For one stand per row, enter zero for pitch and gap. Each stand footprint is centred within the row depth and must not be longer than the bicycle envelope. The drawing checks full envelope fit and counts two nominal positions per stand toward an optional capacity target.

**Example:** Two rows of five stands give **20 nominal positions**. Use **1.90 × 0.65 m** bicycle envelopes (length × width), **0.80 × 0.10 m** stands, **0.075 m** flank gaps and **1.80 m** pitch. This leaves **0.250 m** between loaded stands. With a **2.20 m** aisle, **0.50 m** outer reserves and **1.50 m south / 1.20 m north** head spaces, the whole layout measures **7.000 × 11.450 m**. On a **12 × 16 m** plot, a **2 m west and 2 m south** offset leaves **3.000 m east** and **2.550 m north**. These are example dimensions, not design standards.

The editable DXF separates the layout, clear aisle/head spaces, stand footprints, parked-cycle envelopes and stand IDs in local millimetres. Reopen under **Bicycle rack CAD** in Projects. A failed check keeps the brief, shows a reason and withholds DXF instead of falling back to generic zoning. This is a static fit check, not a loading motion, swept path, accessibility, kerb connection, security, anchoring or shelter design check.
