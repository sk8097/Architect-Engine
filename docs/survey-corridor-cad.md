# Survey corridor CAD

**New Project → Infrastructure / Public Works → Transport infrastructure → Road, Street, Footpath or Cycle Track**. Enter measured length, constant ROW width and survey reference. Choose **Corridor drawing → Survey points: multi-segment corridor CAD**. Under **Site** enter a projected local/grid CRS reference, start chainage in metres and 2–100 consecutive `Easting,Northing` pairs (metres, up to three decimals; separate pairs by a newline or `;`). No coordinate conversion is applied and latitude/longitude is not accepted as the input format. Both the input and the result are plan coordinates, not levels or slopes. Confirm your CRS and coordinate units externally.

Optionally enter `Footway:1.5;Carriageway:6;Footway:1.5` or other measured bands from left to right while looking forward along the points. Total assigned width must not exceed supplied ROW; any unused width stays on the right. ROW is represented symmetrically around the surveyed centreline. Use the band list instead of individual Space selections. Choose Linear Corridor if you select an arrangement.

Save keeps the brief and opens the drawing automatically when valid; reopen through **Projects → Survey corridor CAD**. The app accumulates segment lengths from the entered initial chainage and requires the points to agree with the stated route length to within 0.05 m. Offsets use straight mitred joins; reversals, excessive mitres, folded or overlapping edges and self-intersections stop export. Editable DXF preserves supplied projected grid coordinates in **mm** and has separate centreline, ROW, band and station layers. A band boundary exactly on the centreline uses the existing centreline (no duplicate entity).

The result is a horizontal schematic of the supplied survey, not geometric road or rail design. This straight-segment mode does not interpolate curves. Neither mode generates junctions or transitions, calculates grades, crossfalls, drainage or utilities, or verifies safety, legal boundaries or approvals. The existing straight schematic remains separately available.

## Supplied circular bends

Choose **Corridor drawing → Tangent survey with measured circular bends** for 3–100 controls: the start, one or more intersections of consecutive tangent lines (PI), and the end. These are **not** points sampled along a curve. In Site, enter exactly one positive centreline radius in metres per intermediate control, in the same order, separated by semicolons. For example, `0,0;60,0;60,40` with radius `20` produces a 90° left turn, tangent points at `(40,0)` and `(60,20)`, and a route length of **91.416 m**. The route length must be the straight-tangent lengths plus arc lengths, not the full length of the input control polygon. Both route and control polygon are limited to 20 km.

The radius must exceed half the ROW by at least 1 mm; each bend must turn 0.01°–175° and leave at least 1 mm of tangent between arcs and endpoints. These are adjustable geometry checks, **not engineering minima**. Overlapping arcs, alignment crossings, touching offset boundaries, end caps crossing other parts of the path and mismatched length block export; the brief remains saved. Exact tangents and concentric radius offsets are exported as editable DXF `LINE` and `ARC` entities. Start, PC (point of curvature), PT (point of tangency) and end chainages are labelled along the alignment.

No radii are chosen by the app. Compound/reverse design without a separating tangent, spiral transitions, junctions, profile, superelevation, drainage, sight distance and design-speed checks are outside this template.
