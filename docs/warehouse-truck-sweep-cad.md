# Rigid-truck reverse swept-path CAD

Under **Industrial & Warehousing → Warehouse / logistics**, select a supported single-sided warehouse type and choose **Automatic drawing → Loading apron: rigid truck reverse swept path**. This is a standalone apron study, not the loading hall plan or a cross-dock plan. Leave the separate Space list empty.

Enter the measured plot and cardinal frontage. The rectangular apron begins at the frontage, with its left offset and depth to the dock face supplied. Enter the dock centre measured from the **apron** left, its clear bay width, and the gap from rear bumper to dock when stopped. The vehicle model uses body width including mirrors, wheelbase and front/rear overhangs. The measured turning radius applies to the **rear axle centre**, not the front tyre or wall-to-wall turning circle. Supply a buffer around the vehicle, optional straight lead distance, straight docking distance, and left or right approach looking toward the dock. This tests one path: straight reverse, a 90° constant-radius turn and straight reverse to the stop position. It does not find an alternate or optimal path.

List any fixed rectangular obstacles under Site as `ID,left,front,width,depth` in metres, measured from apron left and frontage; separate entries with semicolons or newlines. The app checks the full buffered body, including swing between the preview poses, against the apron and obstacles, and checks that width and stop gap fit the bay. For the turn, a succession of conservative patches is expanded by the maximum arc deviation over each step. This can block a borderline geometry rather than report it safely clear. The bay lines are guides, not walls; enter adjacent objects as obstacles.

Example geometry, not operational guidance: **48 × 32 m** apron on a **185 × 125 ft** plot, 2 m left offset, dock centre 17 m from apron left, 4 m bay, 0.5 m stop gap; a right approach with 8 m axle radius, 4 m lead and 8 m docking distance. Try 5 m wheelbase, 1 m front and 2 m rear overhang, 2.5 m width and 0.3 m buffer. The axle path is about 24.566 m.

**Save** opens the drawing or a fit blocker and retains the brief. Reopen using **Rigid truck swept-path CAD** in Projects. DXF contains native circular arcs for the axle and buffered corner traces, representative body poses and layers for the apron, dock and obstacles. No articulated vehicles, steering transitions, gradients, dock heights, vehicle access, pedestrian interaction or operational clearance approval are verified.
